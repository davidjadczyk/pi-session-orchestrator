# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and accountable. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not welcome.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through a public repository issue marked as a conduct concern, or contact the maintainers through the public contact route listed by the repository. Do not include credentials, secrets, or private operational data. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate discretion.
